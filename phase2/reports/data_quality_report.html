1. Quality Framework Overview

The automated Data Quality (DQ) framework executes 20 systematic validation checks against the staging and warehouse layers. Each check evaluates a specific data integrity constraint, categorizing the outcome as:

  • PASS — 100% compliance with business rules and integrity constraints.
  • WARN — Tolerable variance within open public datasets (e.g. non-critical optional attributes).
  • FAIL — Zero-tolerance violations (e.g., orphan foreign keys, negative metrics).
